# Role Portal Foundation

## Summary
Add a server-side portal classifier and permission catalogue while preserving legacy admin roles and building scope.

## Scope
- Normalize portal family from authenticated role.
- Expose a non-secret permission catalogue for navigation decisions.
- Preserve policies as the backend authority.
- Defer employee/intern login until a first-class identity relationship exists.

## Validation
Portal mapping and legacy building-admin behavior require feature tests.
